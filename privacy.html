Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Click the Pair is a free emoji matching game that runs in your browser. This policy explains what information is involved when you use the site, and what choices you have. The short version: you do not need an account, we do not ask for your name or email, and the game itself does not send your scores anywhere.

Information the game stores on your device

The game saves one thing: your best score. It is kept in your browser's local storage on your own device so it can be shown the next time you play. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. You can remove it at any time by clearing your browser's site data. If your browser blocks local storage, the game still works, but your best score is not remembered.

Information we do not collect

  • We do not offer accounts, so there are no usernames, passwords or profiles.
  • We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or location.
  • We do not record which tiles you click or how you play.

Sharing your score

The Share button copies a short message to your clipboard or opens your device's own share menu. What you do with that message, and where you send it, is up to you. The game does not see or store it.

Advertising and cookies

The site is supported by advertising provided by Google AdSense. Google and its partners are third-party vendors that use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads.

  • Google uses cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visits to this site and other sites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info/choices.
  • To learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

As part of serving and measuring ads, these advertising partners may collect information such as your IP address, device and browser type, and how you interact with ads. That information is handled under their own privacy policies, not this one.

Where the law requires your consent for advertising cookies, you will be asked for it before those cookies are used, and you can change your choice later. You can also block or delete cookies in your browser settings. The game keeps working if you do.

Analytics

The site uses Google Analytics, loaded through Google Tag Manager, to understand how many people visit and how the site performs. Google Analytics uses cookies and may collect information such as the pages you visit, your device and browser type, your approximate location based on your IP address, and how you interact with the site. Google handles this information under its own privacy policy. You can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Server logs

Like most websites, the company that hosts this site may automatically record basic technical information when a page is requested, such as IP address, browser type, the page requested and the time. This is used to keep the site running and secure.

Children

Click the Pair is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices and rights

Because the game does not collect personal information about you, there is very little for us to access, correct or delete. The best score lives only in your browser, and you control it. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights over data held by advertising partners, which you can exercise through the links above.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the date at the top of this page. Continuing to use the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.
