How the Christmas Board Works

Every tile on this board is part of Christmas. The set has a Christmas tree, Santa and Mrs. Claus, a snowman, a reindeer and an angel, plus presents, bells, a star, stockings, ribbons and a teddy bear. Cookies and milk, candy and chocolate, a scarf, gloves, a sled and carol music notes fill out the rest. Each board shows 12 tiles, and exactly two of them match. Click both to score, and a new board appears.

Christmas emojis lean hard on red, green and gold, so the matching pair hides among close neighbors from the first board on. You have 60 seconds, a wrong click costs 2 seconds, and your best Christmas score is saved separately from the other modes.

A Quick Game for Holiday Parties

A round takes one minute, which makes it an easy party game. Put it on the big screen and let the room shout out the matches, run a quick tournament where the top score wins, or play a round with family while the cookies bake.

After a round, the Share button sends your score with a link that friends can open to try to beat it, which works well in a family group chat. The game runs in any browser, with nothing to download and no account to make.

Tips for the Christmas Board

  • Santa or Mrs. Claus? Both wear red with white trim. Santa has the big white beard, Mrs. Claus has her hair and glasses.
  • Check the winter wear. The scarf, gloves and stocking are all knitted and often the same colors. Look at the shape, not the color.
  • Sort the sweets. Cookies, candy and chocolate tend to turn up together. Spot them first, then compare.
  • Gold is everywhere. The star, the bell and the angel's halo all shine gold. Give each a second look.

Christmas Board FAQ

Which emojis are on the Christmas board?

There are 20 in the set: Christmas tree, Santa, Mrs. Claus, snowman, reindeer, present, bell, star, stocking, cookie, milk, candy, ribbon, scarf, gloves, sled, angel, chocolate, music notes and teddy bear. Each board uses 11 of them, with one appearing twice.

Is this Christmas game free?

Yes. It is free to play in any web browser on a phone, tablet or computer, with no download and no sign-up.

Is it good for a holiday party?

Yes. Rounds are only a minute long, the rules take seconds to explain, and it works on a shared screen or on everyone's own phone.

Is it harder than the classic game?

Usually, yes. With so many reds, greens and golds on every board, it takes a closer look to find the pair than on the mixed classic board.
