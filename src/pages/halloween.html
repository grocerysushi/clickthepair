How the Halloween Board Works

Every tile on this board belongs on Halloween night. The set has a jack-o'-lantern, a ghost, a bat, a skull, a witch, a vampire, a zombie, a wolf, an alien, a clown and an ogre, along with a broom, a crystal ball, theater masks, an owl, a full moon and a crescent moon. Trick-or-treat candy, lollipops and chocolate round it out. Each board shows 12 tiles, and exactly two of them match. Click both to score, and a new board appears.

Spooky characters look alike at a glance, so the matching pair hides among them from the first board on. You have 60 seconds, a wrong click costs 2 seconds, and your best Halloween score is saved separately from the other modes.

A Quick Halloween Party Game

A round lasts one minute, which makes it an easy party game. Put it on the big screen and race to call out the matches, or run a quick tournament where the top score wins the last of the candy.

After a round, the Share button sends your score with a link that friends can open to try to beat it. The game runs in any browser, with nothing to download and no account to make.

Tips for the Halloween Board

  • Two moons. The full moon is a whole circle and the crescent moon is a thin curve. They are not a pair.
  • Sort the monsters. The witch, the vampire and the zombie are all costumed figures. Look for the hat, the cape or the green skin.
  • Check the candy. Candy, lollipops and chocolate often turn up together. Look at the wrapper and the stick.

Halloween Board FAQ

Which emojis are on the Halloween board?

There are 20 in the set: jack-o'-lantern, ghost, bat, skull, witch, vampire, zombie, candy, lollipop, chocolate, full moon, owl, alien, clown, ogre, broom, crystal ball, wolf, crescent moon and theater masks. Each board uses 11 of them, with one appearing twice.

Is this Halloween game free?

Yes. It is free to play in any web browser on a phone, tablet or computer, with no download and no sign-up.

Is it too scary?

No. The emojis are cartoon style, with friendly ghosts and smiling pumpkins, so it is a fun Halloween game rather than a frightening one.
