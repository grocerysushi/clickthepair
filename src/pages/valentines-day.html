How the Valentine's Day Board Works

Every tile on this board is a Valentine. The set has a sparkling heart, a heart with an arrow, a heart wrapped in a ribbon and a love letter, along with roses, tulips, a bouquet, chocolate, candy, strawberries, cherries and a slice of cake. A teddy bear, a ring, a kiss, lipstick, a swan, a heart-eyes face, a ribbon and a present round it out. Each board shows 12 tiles, and exactly two of them match. Click both to score, and a new board appears.

Nearly everything here is red or pink, so the matching pair blends in from the first board on. You have 60 seconds, a wrong click costs 2 seconds, and your best Valentine's Day score is saved separately from the other modes.

Send a Valentine Challenge

Play a round, then use the Share button to send your score to someone special with a link to try to beat it. It is a quick, playful thing to send a partner, a friend or a group chat on February 14.

A round lasts one minute, so it also works as a short break at a Valentine's or Galentine's party. The game runs in any browser, with nothing to download and no account to make.

Tips for the Valentine's Day Board

  • Hearts are the trap. The sparkling heart, the heart with an arrow and the heart with a ribbon are all pink. Look for the sparkles, the arrow or the bow.
  • Sort the red fruit. Strawberries and cherries often turn up together. One has seeds and a leafy top, the other comes as a pair on stems.
  • Flowers differ in shape. The rose is tightly curled, the tulip is a cup, and the bouquet is a wrapped bunch.

Valentine's Day Board FAQ

Which emojis are on the Valentine's Day board?

There are 20 in the set: sparkling heart, heart with arrow, heart with ribbon, love letter, bouquet, rose, chocolate, teddy bear, ring, kiss mark, heart-eyes face, strawberry, cake, ribbon, swan, tulip, present, cherries, lipstick and candy. Each board uses 11 of them, with one appearing twice.

Is this Valentine's Day game free?

Yes. It is free to play in any web browser on a phone, tablet or computer, with no download and no sign-up.

Can I challenge someone to beat my score?

Yes. After a round, press Share to send your score with a link. When they open it, the game shows the score they need to beat.
