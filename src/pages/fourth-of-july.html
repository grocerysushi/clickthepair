How the Fourth of July Board Works

Every tile on this board is part of an Independence Day celebration. The set has fireworks, a sparkler, a firecracker, the Statue of Liberty, an eagle and a star, along with cookout favorites: burgers, hot dogs, fries, drumsticks, corn, watermelon, pie, ice cream and a soda. A balloon, a party popper, a campfire, a baseball cap and a baseball round it out. Each board shows 12 tiles, and exactly two of them match. Click both to score, and a new board appears.

Cookout food shares a lot of browns, reds and yellows, so the matching pair hides among close neighbors from the first board on. You have 60 seconds, a wrong click costs 2 seconds, and your best Fourth of July score is saved separately from the other modes.

A Quick Game While You Wait for Fireworks

A round lasts one minute, which makes it easy to play while the grill heats up or while you wait for dark. Pass a phone around at the cookout and see who finds the most pairs.

After a round, the Share button sends your score with a link that friends can open to try to beat it. The game runs in any browser, with nothing to download and no account to make.

Tips for the Fourth of July Board

  • Fireworks or sparkler? The fireworks burst across the sky. The sparkler is a single stick with a bright starburst.
  • Sort the cookout food. Burgers, hot dogs, fries and drumsticks are all warm browns and yellows. Check their shapes one at a time.
  • Find the blues. The Statue of Liberty and the cap stand out from the reds and browns, so they are quick to check.

Fourth of July Board FAQ

Which emojis are on the Fourth of July board?

There are 20 in the set: fireworks, sparkler, firecracker, Statue of Liberty, eagle, burger, hot dog, watermelon, corn, pie, ice cream, drumstick, balloon, star, party popper, soda, fire, cap, baseball and fries. Each board uses 11 of them, with one appearing twice.

Is this Fourth of July game free?

Yes. It is free to play in any web browser on a phone, tablet or computer, with no download and no sign-up.

Can a group play together?

Yes. Take turns on one device and compare scores, or have everyone play on their own phone and share results. Each player's best score is saved on their own device.
