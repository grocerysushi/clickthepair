How the Thanksgiving Board Works

Every tile on this board belongs on a Thanksgiving table or in a fall afternoon. The set has a turkey and a drumstick, pie, corn, potatoes, sweet potatoes, a casserole, bread, cheese and salad, along with apples, pears, grapes, carrots, peanuts, chestnuts and honey. Maple leaves, fallen leaves and a football round it out. Each board shows 12 tiles, and exactly two of them match. Click both to score, and a new board appears.

Thanksgiving food is full of browns, oranges and golds, so the matching pair hides among close neighbors from the first board on. You have 60 seconds, a wrong click costs 2 seconds, and your best Thanksgiving score is saved separately from the other modes.

A Quick Game for Thanksgiving Day

A round takes one minute, so it fits in while the turkey rests or the pie cools. Pass a phone around the table and see who finds the most pairs, or put it on the TV and call out the matches together. Everyone plays the same rules, and the best score is the one to beat.

Want to challenge someone who is not at the table? After a round, the Share button sends your score with a link they can open to try to beat it. The game runs in any browser, with nothing to download and no account to make.

Tips for the Thanksgiving Board

  • Leaves are a trap. The single maple leaf and the cluster of fallen leaves look alike at a glance. Count the leaves before you click.
  • Sort the browns. Bread, pie, peanuts, chestnuts and the casserole are all warm browns. Check their shapes one at a time.
  • Potato or sweet potato? The plain potato is a light brown oval. The sweet potato is roasted and orange.
  • Turkey and drumstick are different. One is the whole bird, the other is a single cooked leg.

Thanksgiving Board FAQ

Which emojis are on the Thanksgiving board?

There are 20 in the set: turkey, pie, corn, maple leaf, fallen leaves, potato, sweet potato, casserole, apple, pear, grapes, carrot, bread, cheese, drumstick, peanuts, chestnut, honey, football and salad. Each board uses 11 of them, with one appearing twice.

Is this Thanksgiving game free?

Yes. It is free to play in any web browser on a phone, tablet or computer, with no download and no sign-up.

Can a group play together?

Yes. Take turns on one device and compare scores, or have everyone play on their own phone and share results. Each player's best score is saved on their own device.

Is it harder than the classic game?

Usually, yes. With so many fall browns and oranges on every board, it takes a closer look to find the pair than on the mixed classic board.
