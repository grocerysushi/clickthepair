How Easy Mode Works

Easy Mode is the gentlest way to play Click the Pair. The board has just nine big tiles, three across and three down, and the emojis on each board are picked from the whole collection, so neighboring tiles are rarely alike. Exactly two tiles match. Find them, click both, and a fresh board appears.

There are no time penalties. A wrong click makes the tile wiggle, and that is all: the clock keeps running at its normal pace. You still have 60 seconds to find as many pairs as you can, and your Easy Mode best score is saved separately from the other modes.

Who Easy Mode Is For

Easy Mode suits anyone who wants a calmer round. It is a good first game for people who are new to Click the Pair, a warm-up before trying the classic board or the daily challenge, and a comfortable choice on a small phone screen, where bigger tiles are easier to hit. Because the tiles are large and a wrong click costs nothing, it also works well when two people are playing together on one screen and taking turns.

Tips for Easy Mode

  • Go for speed. With no penalty, a quick click on a likely pair costs nothing if you are wrong, so trust your first impression.
  • Read the board in rows. Three short rows are quick to sweep from left to right.
  • Move up when it feels easy. Once you are regularly finding more than 25 pairs, the classic game and the daily challenge will feel like a fair test.

Easy Mode FAQ

Does a wrong click do anything in Easy Mode?

It makes the tile wiggle, but it does not take any time off the clock.

Is my Easy Mode score saved?

Yes. Your best score for Easy Mode is stored in your browser on this device, separately from your classic and Hard Mode scores. It is not sent anywhere.

Can I play Easy Mode on a tablet?

Yes. The nine large tiles work especially well on tablets and phones, and the game also works with a mouse or a keyboard.
